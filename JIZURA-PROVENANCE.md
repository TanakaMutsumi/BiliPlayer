## What is included

- `public/vendor/jizura/src/` — JIZURA engine sources used by BiliPlayer
- `public/vendor/jizura/jizura-engine.js` — concatenated engine bundle, generated by
  `scripts/build-jizura-engine.mjs`
- `public/js/jizura-bridge.js` — BiliPlayer integration (lazy load, LRC conversion, plan,
  canvas rendering, presets, seed history)
- `public/css/jizura.css`, `public/css/jizura-fonts.css` — host and font styles
- `public/fonts/jizura/` — bundled display-font subsets
